### Notes on using this template

- Keep the whole thing to a few lines at the end of the work. This is a closing note, not a
  document.
- "What worked" is not courtesy. Naming an effective practice specifically is what keeps it
  from being revised away later.
- A retrospective whose conclusion is "nothing should change" is a success, not a failure to
  find something.
- Never include a proposed rule and the edit in the same breath. Propose, wait, then draft.
